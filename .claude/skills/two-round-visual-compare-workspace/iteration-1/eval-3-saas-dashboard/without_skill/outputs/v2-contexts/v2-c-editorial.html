The week that was

Mon 8 Jun — Sun 14 Jun · week 24

For a quarter that started with a flat forecast, the closing stretch has been a study in execution. Maple & Finch, the procurement-led enterprise account that reps have been chasing for fourteen months, finally turned the corner at 09:14 on Monday morning — proposal accepted, awaiting legal redlines. The $182,000 deal is the largest single new logo of the year.

Cedar Robotics, which slipped from a "should-close" to "will-likely-slip" in the back half of May, was rescued by a late add of four seats pushed through by R. Okafor. The deal re-closed at $96,500, up from an original $86,000. Net of credits, this is the cleanest expansion of the quarter.

"The numbers tell a story: when the team protects at-risk renewals instead of chasing net new, both line items move." — L. Tanaka, Senior AE

Halberd Logistics remains the one live disappointment. The decision committee postponed on Sunday evening; the deal sits paused until July 2. With $58,200 of weighted pipeline resting on a single committee meeting, the team is rightly nervous. The mitigant: a clean Discovery-stage pipeline that is the strongest in three quarters.
